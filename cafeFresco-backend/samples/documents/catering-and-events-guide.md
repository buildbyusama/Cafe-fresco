# Cafe Fresco Catering and Events Guide

Version 1.0 · Applies to all catering and event bookings

## Overview

Cafe Fresco caters weddings, birthdays, corporate events and family gatherings. We cook everything fresh in our own kitchen on the day of the event. Menus are built from our regular dishes, so customers can taste any dish in the cafe before booking.

## Weddings and large functions

We cater weddings and functions for 50 to 400 guests. Bookings must be made at least fourteen days before the event. A deposit of thirty percent of the agreed quote confirms the date; the balance is paid on the event day before service starts.

Our team arrives two hours before guests to set up the buffet, warmers and serving stations, and clears everything away after the event. One server is provided for every 25 guests. Crockery, cutlery and table linen are included.

A tasting session for up to four people can be arranged once the deposit is paid.

## Birthday parties

The party hall at the cafe seats up to 40 children or 30 adults. Birthday bookings need at least two days notice. Every party package includes balloons, a decorated cake table and a host who runs games for children. Customers may bring their own cake; a cake-cutting service is included.

Parties run for up to three hours. Extra time can be added if the hall is free.

## Corporate and office catering

We deliver boxed lunches to offices for teams of 10 or more on weekdays. Orders placed by 10 am are delivered for lunch the same day. Each box is labelled with the person's name and any dietary note. Recurring weekly orders and monthly invoicing are available for companies.

For conferences and seminars we provide tea and snack breaks as well as buffet lunches.

## Menu planning

Catering menus can include starters, main dishes, rice, bread, desserts and drinks from our regular menu. We can prepare vegetarian and mild options on request. For prices, ask our staff for a written quote: catering is priced per guest and depends on the menu chosen.

## Changes and cancellations

Guest numbers can be changed up to five days before the event. Cancellations made more than seven days before the event receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made within seven days are refunded at fifty percent of the deposit, because food and staff are already arranged.

## How to book

Use the Contact page or speak to the manager at the cafe. Please share the date, the expected number of guests, the venue and any dietary needs.
