# Cafe Fresco Customer FAQ

Version 1.0 · Clear answers to the questions customers ask most

## Do I need an account to order?

No. You can order as a guest. An account lets you see past orders, track orders, save addresses and get recommendations based on what you like.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login page, choose "Forgot password" and enter your email. You will receive a link to set a new password. The link works for a limited time; if it expires, request a new one.

## How do I track my order?

Open My Orders after logging in, or ask the assistant with your order number. The status shows Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Out for delivery or Delivered.

## Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Only while the order is still Pending. Cancel the order from My Orders and place it again with the right address. Once the kitchen is preparing it, contact us through the Contact page straight away.

## Can I schedule an order for later?

Online orders are prepared as soon as they are placed. For a specific time, such as an office lunch or a party, use our catering service and book in advance.

## What if my food arrives cold, late or wrong?

Tell us within 24 hours with your order number and, if possible, a photo. We will replace the item or refund it. You can report it through the Contact page or ask the assistant to pass it to our staff.

## How do refunds work?

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded as store credit or by bank transfer.

## Do you offer gift cards or a loyalty programme?

Not at the moment. Current offers and discount codes are shown on the Deals section of the website.

## Do you take large or bulk orders?

Yes. Orders for 10 or more people are handled by our catering team so the food arrives together and on time. See the Catering and Events Guide or use the Contact page.

## Is the food suitable for vegetarians?

Yes, several dishes are vegetarian and are cooked separately from meat dishes. Use the vegetarian filter on the menu, or ask the assistant for vegetarian dishes.

## Can I ask for less spicy food?

Most curries can be made mild. Add a note when ordering or ask staff in the cafe. Some marinated dishes are prepared in advance and cannot be changed.

## How do you use my personal data?

We use your name, phone number and address only to prepare and deliver your orders and to contact you about them. We do not sell your data. Chat conversations with the assistant are kept for 30 days to improve the service. Never share card details in the chat.

## How do I delete my account?

Send a request through the Contact page from the email address on your account. We will delete your account and personal data, except order records we must keep for accounting.

## Do you have job openings?

We hire kitchen staff, servers and delivery riders from time to time. Send your name, the role you are interested in and your experience through the Contact page.

## How can I give feedback?

Use the thumbs up or down on any assistant answer, the Contact page, or speak to the manager in the cafe. Every comment is read by our team.
