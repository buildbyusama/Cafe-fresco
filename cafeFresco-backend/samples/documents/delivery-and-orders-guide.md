# Cafe Fresco Delivery and Orders Guide

Version 1.0 · For online orders placed on the Cafe Fresco website

## Placing an order

Customers can order from the website as a guest or with an account. An account keeps an order history, saved addresses and order tracking. Every order receives a short order number that can be used to track it.

## Preparation and delivery times

Most orders are prepared within 20 to 30 minutes. Delivery usually takes a further 20 to 40 minutes depending on distance and traffic. During busy evenings and public holidays it can take longer; the order page shows the latest status.

## Order status

Each order moves through these steps: Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Out for delivery and Delivered. Customers can follow these steps on the My Orders page or ask the assistant with their order number.

## Packaging

Hot and cold dishes are packed separately. Curries and gravies are sealed in leak-proof containers. We use recyclable boxes where possible and do not add plastic cutlery unless the customer asks for it.

## Changing or cancelling an order

An order can be cancelled from My Orders while it is still Pending. Once the kitchen has started preparing it, the order cannot be changed or cancelled; please contact us through the Contact page as soon as possible if something is wrong.

## Problems with an order

If an item is missing, wrong or not up to standard, tell us within 24 hours through the Contact page or the assistant, with the order number and a photo if possible. We will replace the item on the next order or refund it, depending on what the customer prefers.

## Collecting from the cafe

Customers can collect their order from the counter. Please bring the order number. Orders not collected within one hour of being ready may no longer be at their best quality.
