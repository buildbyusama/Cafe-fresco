# Cafe Fresco Food Quality and Safety

Version 1.0 · How we prepare and handle food

## Fresh preparation

Sauces, marinades and dough are made in our kitchen every morning. Vegetables are delivered fresh six days a week. We do not use frozen ready meals.

## Kitchen hygiene

Our kitchen staff hold food-handling training certificates and are retrained every year. Surfaces are cleaned and sanitised between every shift. Raw meat, cooked food and vegetables use separate colour-coded boards and knives.

## Temperature control

Cold food is stored below 5 °C and hot food is held above 63 °C. Fridge and freezer temperatures are checked and recorded twice a day. Cooked food that is not served within two hours of being cooked is not sold.

## Oil and frying

Frying oil is filtered every day and replaced regularly. We use vegetable oil and do not use reused oil from other kitchens.

## Spice levels

Dishes marked spicy contain green or red chilli. Most curries can be made mild on request; ask before ordering, because some marinades are prepared in advance and cannot be changed.

## Feedback

We read every comment left through the Contact page and the assistant. If a dish is not right, tell us and we will make it right.
