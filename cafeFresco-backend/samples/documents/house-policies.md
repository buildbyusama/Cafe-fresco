# Cafe Fresco House Policies

Version 1.0 · For customers dining in, ordering online or collecting orders

## Halal and ingredients

All meat served at Cafe Fresco is halal and comes from certified suppliers. We do not serve pork or alcohol, and no alcohol is used in cooking.

## Allergies and dietary needs

Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and sesame. We label dishes where we can, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from allergens. Customers with a serious allergy should speak to the manager before ordering. Vegetarian dishes are cooked separately from meat dishes.

## Children

High chairs and a small kids corner with crayons and books are available. Baby-changing facilities are in the ground-floor washroom. Children must be supervised by an adult in the kids corner.

## Parking and access

Free parking for around 30 cars is behind the building. Valet parking is offered on Friday and Saturday evenings. The ground floor is wheelchair accessible, including one washroom; the party hall is on the first floor and is reached by stairs.

## Seating and reservations

Walk-in customers are seated in order of arrival. Tables for groups of eight or more should be reserved at least one day ahead through the Contact page. A reserved table is held for fifteen minutes after the booking time.

## Wi-Fi and working

Free Wi-Fi is available for dining customers; ask staff for the password. Laptops are welcome, but during busy lunch and dinner times we may ask customers to share large tables.

## Pets and smoking

Pets are not allowed inside, except assistance dogs. Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is not allowed anywhere inside the cafe.

## Lost property

Items left at the cafe are kept at the counter for thirty days. After thirty days, unclaimed items are donated to charity.
