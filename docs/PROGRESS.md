## Backend contract now live

- `POST /order/create {shippingAddress, discountCode?, paymentMethod:'card'|'cod'}` builds the order from the **server cart**. `products`, `totalAmount` and `payment` are ignored. COD creates its Payment in the same request and sends the email. The cart is emptied.
- `services/orderService.js` holds `createOrderFromCart`, `cancelOrder`, `markOrderPaid`, `cancelStaleUnpaidCardOrders` and `sweepStaleOrders`. The sweep runs lazily in create, fetch, user/history and payment-intent.
- `utils/price.js` (`round2`, `unitPrice`, `computeLines`), `utils/validation.js`, `utils/cookies.js`, `middleware/loginLimiter.js` (`createLimiter`), `services/inventoryService.js` (`adjustStock`, `setStock`, `reserveItems`, `restoreItems`) and `services/discountService.js`.
- The order model adds `paymentMethod` (`card`|`cod`), `shippingAddress`, `stripePaymentIntentId`, `idempotencyKey` (unique per user), `stockRestored` and `statusHistory {byType, byId}`.
- `GET /order/history/:userId` is admin/manager only. `/order/stats` is removed. `/order/fetch` returns `{total, totalPages}`, newest first, and 200 when empty.
- Deprecated but still working until B.6: `/order/set-transaction`, `/order/send-order-email`, `/stripe/payment/create`, and the `newTotal` alias in `/discount/apply`. **The shop no longer calls any of them.**
- Migration: `node scripts/migrateDiscountCodes.js`. It is idempotent and lowercases `paymentMethod`.
